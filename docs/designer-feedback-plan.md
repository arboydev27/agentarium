# Designer feedback plan

A game designer reviewed the colony after the five-phase [visual rebuild](visual-rebuild-plan.md) shipped (October 2026) and returned three findings:

1. **Immersion.** Beyond the agent residents the colony needs other activity: bots or workers doing relatable things, rovers wandering, individuals with visible relationships to the place.
2. **Stillness and interactivity.** The world looks too still, and it needs meaningful interactivity so people can explore rather than only watch camera presets.
3. **Textures.** Texture quality still lags in many places on the map; consider pre-built Blender presets or asset packs instead of only the project's own generated materials.

This plan diagnoses each finding against the renderer as it exists, lists candidate approaches with their cost against the frame budget and the kit cap, recommends one, and splits the recommendation into phases small enough to commit one at a time. Nothing here touches the bridge, session identity, attention, task briefs or the simulator. Every new actor is decorative and every new mechanic is navigational: none of it reports or implies provider work, and no ambient figure can be mistaken for a session.

## Budget and guardrails

These numbers come from [Rendering performance](rendering-performance.md) (embedded development browser inside the Claude desktop app, 1024 × 768, eight simulated residents, daylight, settled single samples on a 120 Hz display that caps readings at 120 fps). Every phase below is measured the same way before and after, and the result goes into that document.

| ------------------------------ | ------- | ------- | ------ | --------- |
| Overview                       | high    | 66      | 17.5   | 1,973     |
| Overview                       | low     | 120     | 9.6    | 1,422     |

| Horizon                        | high    | 65      | 21.9   | 2,089     |
| The other eight landmark shots | high    | 118–120 | ≤ 10.3 | 585–1,088 |

Rules this work holds to:

- **Frame.** The Overview stays at or above 60 fps with a p95 at or below 18 ms and gains no more than about 3% draws per phase (about 60). Each landmark shot stays within 3 fps of its table value. Low quality stays at 120 fps everywhere. A phase that cannot hold this is cut back, not merged.
- **Assets.** The kit GLB is 7,455,696 bytes against the 10 MiB (10,485,760 byte) test cap, leaving 3,030,064 bytes. The colonist is 1,306,312 bytes. The fifteen KTX2 surface maps total 1,705,764 bytes, of which the five UASTC normals are 1,287,155. Proposed texture ceiling for this round: **8 MB of KTX2 in total**, so textures never cost more than the kit geometry does.
- **Licensing.** Any downloaded asset needs the owner's explicit OK before it is fetched, must be CC0 or an equally permissive licence, goes into its own fresh directory, and is recorded in `public/models/LICENSE.md` or a new `public/textures/LICENSE.md`.
- **Ambient actors.** No roster entry, no label, no selection, no attention state, no store writes. A different silhouette, scale or palette from the colonist so a figure is never read as a session. They stay off the reserved resident lanes, seats, rest spots and destination branches by a tested margin; nothing in this round joins the `NavigationTraffic` lease scheme, because a slow ambient actor holding a corridor would delay the one thing residents do that the owner most wants seen, their outings.
- **Accessibility.** Reduced motion parks every actor and makes every camera move immediate; every mechanic works from the keyboard; low quality keeps behaviour that reads as world behaviour and drops only particles.
- **Verification per phase.** Tests in the matching `*.test.ts`, then `npx vitest run src`, `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx prettier --check .`; the dev server from `.claude/launch.json` (`agentarium-dev`) with `?renderStats=1`, measuring the Overview and the affected shots at high and low quality; screenshots into `docs/review/designer-feedback/`; updates to `docs/3d-world.md`, `docs/world-layout.md`, `docs/rendering-performance.md`, `docs/roadmap.md`, `README.md` and the status section at the end of this file. Commits happen only when the owner asks, in the owner's identity, with no AI attribution. The baseline before any change: 261 tests pass.

## 1. Immersion: a colony with only eight people in it

### Why it reads that way

- **Every moving figure is an agent.** The only human-scale bodies are the eight residents, so the viewer learns that a figure means a session, and the place reads as an office with nobody else in it. Nothing works here except the agents.
- **Decorative motion is three things, and none is in the default shot.** The spaceport rover loop, the ninety-second rocket cycle and the dust field (`src/world/ambientLife.ts`, `src/world/ambient/AmbientLife.tsx`, `src/world/scenery/NorthstarOutpost.tsx`). The Overview, which is what people see first, moves only through residents and faint dust.
- **Landmarks are props.** The telescope never slews, the roof dish never tracks, the solar racks, masts and relay are frozen, nothing is under construction, nothing is carried anywhere. "Visible relationships to the place" means machines and figures whose actions explain what the buildings are for.
- **The constraints that shaped this.** The kit joins every module by material at export, so sub-parts (wheels, a dish, a telescope barrel) cannot articulate on their own; a whole module can turn, drive or hover. Resident lanes are reserved corridors (`src/world/navigation.ts`), and the reservation scheme is deliberately conservative. Each colonist rig costs eleven draws and an animation mixer.

## 2. Stillness and interactivity: nothing answers the viewer

### Why it reads that way

- **Still.** Beyond the residents, the default shot moves only through the dust field and the slow beacon pulse; the dollhouse reveal reacts to camera distance, but nothing reacts to the person. The phases under finding 1 supply the motion; this section is about agency.
- **What exists.** Presets on the hotbar and number keys, orbit, zoom and arrow-key pan, map scouting (clicking the map and **Move camera here** already flies the camera), follow, watch mode, the day/evening toggle, and clicking a resident. That is a complete passive toolset and almost no active one.
- **Nothing in the scene except residents responds.** `WorldKit` and `KitInstances` replace `raycast` with a no-op, so modules, landmarks and machines swallow clicks and `onPointerMissed` clears the selection. There is no way to learn what a place is, who is there, or go there from the world itself.
- **The camera cannot get close.** Orbit stops at 4 m and the polar limit keeps it above the target plane, but hills can still put the eye under the ground on low pitches, so near-ground views are not safe today.

## 3. Textures: the maps are better than what the shader does with them

### Why it reads that way

- **Five 512 px procedural families.** `assets/blender/build_habitat.py` writes ceramic-paint, dusty-alloy, cast-foundation, ironstone and regolith at `N=512` from deterministic noise. Noise has no macro structure, so every surface shares one grain.
- **The triplanar path discards most of each map.** Every kit module, every primitive, the residents' suits and every rock and landform go through `applySurfaceDetails` in `src/world/WorldSurfaceMaterial.tsx`. It reads only the red channel of the colour map, as a brightness multiplier, and the green channel of the roughness map, and derives a faint bump from the grain's screen-space derivatives. **The normal map is never sampled**; `kitMaterials.ts` even nulls `normalMap`. The visible result is flat base colour times greyscale noise. The five UASTC normals, 1,287,155 bytes or 75% of the texture payload, are downloaded, transcoded and uploaded, and only the Expedition Base ground patch (`ReferenceHabitat.tsx`) ever samples one.
- **Where real UV textures exist it looks right.** The Expedition Base reference GLB carries its own UV-mapped maps and is the designer-approved material reference. Everything else gets the grain path. That gap is "lags behind in many places".
- **The ground is a separate pipeline** (`src/world/terrainBake.ts`, `TerrainMaterial.ts`): three layers baked from noise at start-up (512 px at high quality, 256 at low) and tiled at 2.3 to 4.6 m, about 110 to 220 texels per metre. Fine beyond 5 m, soft in the close dollhouse shots, and the explore camera in X-2 will make that more visible.
- **Resolution is not the limit in the landmark shots.** At the 40 m shot reach, 25 to 34 m of world cross 1024 px, about 30 to 40 px per metre on screen. What is missing there is material response: relief, roughness variation and colour variation at the metre scale.

## Suggested order

Each item is one commit. The order alternates visible wins across the three findings and puts the cheapest, most measurable work first, so later phases are judged on a scene that already has relief and motion.

1. **T-1** Use the maps we ship.
2. **X-1** Places you can click.
3. **I-1** Machines at work.
4. **I-2** Maintenance drones.
5. **T-2** Scanned ground (needs the download OK).
6. **I-3** Construction site and crew.
7. **X-2** Explore camera.
8. Optional, in this order if wanted: **I-4** crew clips, **T-3** scanned kit finishes, **X-3** time of day, **I-5** Biolab routine, **X-4** reactive airlocks, **T-4** hero-module detail.

## Decisions for the owner

1. Which phases, in which order. The default is the list above.
2. Whether CC0 texture downloads are allowed (T-2, T-3), and from which source. The exact sets, resolutions and sizes will be named before anything is fetched.
3. Whether Blender may regenerate `public/models/resident/colonist.glb` for I-4. No recommended phase needs `build_world_kit.py`, so the reference habitat outputs are not touched.
4. The texture payload ceiling: 8 MB of KTX2 proposed.
5. Where the construction site goes (west of Habitat Commons beyond the yard tanks is proposed) and whether the crew may appear in the Overview, where it costs about 50 draws.

## Status

Baseline on this branch before any phase: 261 tests pass, the kit GLB is 7,455,696 bytes, the KTX2 payload is 1,705,764 bytes, and the frame numbers are the Phase 5 table in [Rendering performance](rendering-performance.md).

### T-1 Use the maps we ship: implemented (October 31, 2026)

- **Shipped.** `src/world/surfaceShader.ts` is a new pure module that replaces the grain-only hook that lived in `WorldSurfaceMaterial.tsx`. It projects the colour, normal and roughness maps along three axes, drops projections under 2% weight and samples the rest with explicit gradients, reads the colour map relative to the family's authored base colour (`SURFACE_BASE_COLORS`) at a per-family contrast (`SURFACE_CONTRAST`), adds a macro lookup at 7.3 times the grain scale, perturbs the shading normal with the tangent-space normal map through a per-projection derivative tangent frame at a per-family strength (`SURFACE_NORMAL_SCALE`), and keeps the old path's mean brightness per family (`surfaceGain`). `WorldSurfaceMaterial`, the kit materials, `RockMaterial` (which now passes the baked basalt layer's mean colour) and the resident suits call it with an options object.
- **Tests.** `src/world/surfaceShader.test.ts` compiles the hooks against a stub shader and checks that every map is bound and sampled in all three projections, that the three.js include anchors survive, that local and world modes differ in projection space and cache key, that the foundation joints are gated, that the per-family base is darker in linear than its authored sRGB value and the gain matches the old mean, and that `textureMeanColor` decodes sRGB. The suite is at 267 tests.
- **Measured.** In the headless Metal harness described in [Rendering performance](rendering-performance.md#designer-feedback-t-1-surface-maps-in-the-triplanar-shader-october-31-2026), the final shader is within two fps of the old one on the Overview (66 against 64), Habitat Commons (91 against 92), Expedition Base (106 against 108), Northstar (119 against 119) and Horizon (63 against 63). The first version, which fetched all three projections unconditionally, cost 6 fps at the Overview and 8 at the Horizon; skipping the near-zero projections recovered all of it. Draw counts are unchanged.
- **Captures.** `docs/review/designer-feedback/t1-before-*.jpg` and `t1-after-*.jpg` for the Overview, Expedition Base, Northstar and Habitat Commons, 1024 × 768, headless Chrome, `?lookdev=` so the frame is the same.
- **Observed, not fixed here.** The cast-foundation colour map's low-frequency sine banding reads as planks at its 0.45 m repeat on the colony decks under the old shader and the new one alike; the new path holds that family at 0.4 contrast so it is no worse. It is a property of the generated map, which is what T-2 and T-3 replace. Because the colour map is now read relative to the family base, any future change to a family's base colour in `build_habitat.py` must be mirrored in `SURFACE_BASE_COLORS`.

### X-1 Places you can click: implemented (October 31, 2026)
