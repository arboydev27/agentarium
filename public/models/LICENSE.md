# RobotExpressive

Model by [Tomás Laulhé](https://www.patreon.com/quaternius). Before using this
model on a project, consider supporting the creator's Patreon. CC0 1.0.

Modifications by [Don McCurdy](https://donmccurdy.com/):

- Added three facial expression morph targets
- Converted with FBX2GLTF
- Removed duplicate materials and reduced material metalness

## Agentarium additions

The GLB is unchanged. Agentarium adds original procedural accessory geometry in `src/world/appearance.ts` and authored desk/destination animation clips in `src/world/rig.ts` at runtime. These additions do not introduce third-party asset downloads or replace the model attribution above.
