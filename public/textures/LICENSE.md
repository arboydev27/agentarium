# Bundled textures

## `habitat/`

Original Agentarium assets. The five material families (worn ceramic paint,
dusty alloy, cast foundation, eroded ironstone, regolith) are generated by
`assets/blender/build_habitat.py` and encoded to KTX2 by
`scripts/encode-textures.sh`. No third-party data is involved.

## `ground/`

The terrain's three splat layers use scanned sets from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), all released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
`scripts/ground-textures.sh` downloads each at 1K (colour as JPG, OpenGL-convention
normal as 16-bit PNG, ambient-occlusion/roughness/metalness as JPG), verifies the
files against their SHA-256 sums and encodes them to KTX2; the downloaded sources
are not committed. The shader reads each scan as variation around its own mean
colour and re-bases it onto the baked layer's Mars palette
(`src/world/groundSets.ts`), so the shipped colours are not the scans' own.
